🐱 Cat settings

Tell the cat what you're up to, and how much of a nuisance to be.

Permission needed

The cat can't see which window is in front, so it can't tell work from slacking. Allow it under Privacy & Security → Accessibility, then come back.

Focus mode

Watch my screen and nag me
Off by default. The cat is just a pet: it never looks at your screen, needs no permissions, and sends nothing anywhere.
Switch it on and the cat checks whether the window in front of you matches your goal, and pesters you when it doesn't. That needs the Accessibility permission, an internet connection and your license key.

Your goal

Be specific: "practice Spanish, YouTube lessons are fine" works better than "study".
Leave it empty and the cat stops watching, even with focus mode on.

Focus mode key

Focus mode asks TypeSafe's Jev model whether your window matches your goal. Make a key at typesafe.ai — it's about $0.00002 per check, so a month of heavy use costs a few cents.
Without a key the cat still nags you, but only from a short built-in list of distracting sites. The key is stored on this computer and sent only to TypeSafe.

Only the app name and window title are checked, and only when they change. Nothing is stored and no screenshots are taken.
